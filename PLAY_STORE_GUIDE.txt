QURAN PLAYER - HOW TO PUBLISH ON GOOGLE PLAY (step by step)

Be ready for about 3 to 4 weeks. Most of it is waiting for Google's rules.
Last checked against Google's pages on 20 Nov 2026. Rules change, so read what Play Console shows you.

PART A - BUILD THE FILE GOOGLE WANTS
1. Update your website first (quran-website-files.zip): upload index.html, privacy.html, sw.js
   to your GitHub website repository. Open privacy.html and replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with your real email.
   Your policy link will be: https://majeed-creator.github.io/QURAN-RECITATION-/privacy.html
2. On github.com make a NEW repository named quran-android and choose PRIVATE.
   (Private is important: your signing key will be stored in it.)
3. Upload the folder "app" and the files build.gradle, settings.gradle, gradle.properties
   (from quran-android-playstore.zip).
4. Add file > Create new file. Name: .github/workflows/build-release.yml
   Paste the text of build-release.yml from the zip. Commit.
5. Actions tab > "Build Play Store bundle and test APK" > Run workflow.
   Type a password (6+ characters) and WRITE IT DOWN. Use the same password every time.
6. After 5 to 15 minutes open the run and download 3 files under Artifacts:
   - play-store-bundle-aab  (this is what you upload to Google Play)
   - test-apk               (install this on your phone or TV to test first)
   - upload-key-backup      (save this somewhere safe, with your password)
7. If the run fails, open it, copy the red error text and send it to Claude.

PART B - GOOGLE PLAY ACCOUNT
1. Go to play.google.com/console. Sign in with a Google account. Choose Personal account.
   Pay the one-time registration fee (US$25 when I checked). Complete identity verification.
   Check at sign-up that your country and payment card are accepted.
2. A personal account made after 13 Nov 2023 must run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days in a row
   before production. Organization accounts skip this but need company details.

PART C - CREATE THE APP IN PLAY CONSOLE
1. Create app: name "Quran Player", language English, App, Free.
2. Dashboard tasks - use the answers in STORE_LISTING.txt for:
   - Privacy policy URL, App access (all features available without login), Ads (No),
     Content rating questionnaire, Target audience, Data safety, Foreground service declaration,
     Location permission declaration.
3. Main store listing: paste texts from STORE_LISTING.txt. Upload graphics from the graphics folder:
   app icon 512x512, feature graphic 1024x500. Add at least 2 phone screenshots (take them from the test APK).
4. To appear on Android TV: Setup > Advanced settings > Form factors > add Android TV.
   Upload the TV banner 1280x720 and TV screenshots (1920x1080). Google reviews TV apps for remote-control use.

PART D - TEST, THEN RELEASE
1. Testing > Closed testing > Create track > upload app-release.aab > add testers (12 or more email
   addresses of Google accounts, or a Google Group) > Start rollout.
2. Ask each tester to accept the invite, install the app and open it on several days.
   They must stay opted in for 14 days in a row.
3. After 14 days press Apply for production, answer the questions, then create a Production release
   with the same or a newer bundle. Each new upload needs a higher version number. The workflow does this
   automatically on every run.
4. Review can take up to about 7 days.

THINGS THAT CAN GET THE APP REJECTED (read before you submit)
- "Website in an app": Google can reject apps that only show a website. This app bundles its pages and adds
  native features (location, file picker, background service), which helps but gives no guarantee.
- Audio and text come from mp3quran.net, quran.com, alquran.cloud and Open-Meteo. Read their terms for use in
  a store app, or ask them for permission, before you submit. I have not read all of them.
- The background service shows a permanent notification while the app is open. Google asks you to explain
  media-playback services. Use the text in STORE_LISTING.txt.
- Never make the quran-android repository public, and never lose your password and key backup.
  Google Play keeps your real signing key, so a lost upload key can be reset through Play Console support.
