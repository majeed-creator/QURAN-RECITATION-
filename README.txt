QURAN TV APP - HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR ANDROID TV

1. Host the 4 files (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icons) on a free HTTPS site:
   - Go to github.com, make a free account, create a new public repository.
   - Upload all files from this folder (Add file > Upload files).
   - Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", branch "main", folder "/ (root)". Save.
   - After a minute your site is live at https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/

2. Test it: open that link on your TV browser (TV Bro or Puffin TV) and press Start.

3. Make an APK (optional): go to pwabuilder.com, paste your link, choose
   Package for stores > Android, download the zip and take the .apk file.
   Copy it to the TV with "Send Files to TV" or a USB stick and install it.
   Note: this APK may not appear on the TV home screen. Open it with a free
   app such as "Sideload Launcher" from the Play Store.
