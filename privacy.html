Quran Player – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Quran Player is a free app and website that plays Quran recitation, shows verses with translation, and pauses for prayer times. This policy explains what information the app uses.

What we do not do

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app. We do not run a server that stores your information. We do not sell or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (reciter, surah, favorites, schedule, language, prayer-time adjustments), your chosen location, and any files you save for offline use or add as an azan sound are stored only on your device. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Location

If you tap “Use my location”, the app asks for permission and reads your approximate location to calculate prayer times. The coordinates are used on your device and are not sent to us. You can instead search for a city or pick one from the list.

Third-party services

To work, the app contacts these services directly from your device. They can see your IP address and the request you make, under their own privacy policies:

  • mp3quran.net and quran.com: Quran recitation audio and verse timing.
  • alquran.cloud: Quran text, translations and tafseer.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): city search. The city name you type is sent to this service.
  • Google Fonts: fonts used for Arabic and Urdu text.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
