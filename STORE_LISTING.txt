SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80): Quran recitation that pauses for azan. Urdu translation, highlighted verses.

FULL DESCRIPTION (English):
Listen to the Quran all day with a player that respects prayer times.

* Pauses automatically for each azan and for the prohibited prayer times, then resumes where it stopped.
* Verse-by-verse highlighting while the recitation plays, with Urdu or English translation and optional Arabic tafseer.
* Famous reciters, including Mishary Alafasy, Sudais, Shuraim, Abdul Basit, Husary, Minshawi and more.
* Choose any surah, repeat it, or continue to the next. Set the hours you want it to play.
* Prayer times for your location: use GPS or search any city. Multiple calculation methods and a Friday Jumu'ah pause.
* Add your own azan sound. The Quran pauses, your azan plays, and the Quran resumes.
* Daily plan: for example play Surah Al-Kahf every Friday morning.
* Sleep timer, favorites, volume control, Hijri date and a countdown to the next prayer.
* Save surahs for offline listening.
* Works on phones and Android TV. Urdu and English screens.
Free. No ads. No account.

Prayer times are calculated and may differ by a few minutes from your local mosque. You can adjust each prayer.
